About

Simple is a deliverable

Good design isn't decoration. It's the shortest path between a confused user and a confident one.

I didn't start in product. My first decade was visual — match-day creative for cricket tournaments watched by millions, airline campaigns, brand identities. That craft never left. It's why my interfaces hold together at a glance, not just under a spec.

Today I work where the stakes are highest: healthcare and enterprise software, where a confusing screen has real consequences. Hand me regulation, clinical nuance, and twenty-year-old workflow, and you get back flows, prototypes, and production-ready UI — plus the design systems that keep your whole team shipping in sync. I've folded AI into the work too, certified across AI for UX, agents, and Claude workflows, so you get the speed without losing the judgment.
