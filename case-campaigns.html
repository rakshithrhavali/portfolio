Creative for a billion cricket fans

Match day creative and sponsorship decks for some of the loudest rooms in sport, including IPL, PSL, Asia Cup and the Rugby World Cup, turned around on deadlines measured in overs. This is where I learned to make complicated things look effortless, and to do it fast.

When your audience runs into the hundreds of millions and the deadline is measured in overs, you can't afford to be precious. The work has to be sharp, on brand and out the door.

The challenge

Enormous stakes, impossible clock

Live sport doesn't wait for anyone. Every match throws up new moments by the minute, and each one is a chance for a brand to show up, but only if the creative is ready before the moment passes. Multiply that across tournaments running at the same time, several sponsors and every social format, and the real design problem becomes volume.

The obvious trap is giving up quality for speed, or missing the deadline to protect quality. My job was to avoid making that trade at all.

The approach

Systemise the speed

  1. Template the repeatable

    Built flexible, on brand templates for the formats that came up every match, so each asset started at 70% instead of from scratch.

  2. Protect the brand

    Locked down the things that couldn't change, like logo, colour, type and clear space, so moving fast never meant the brand drifted across sponsors.

  3. Turn it around live

    Produced match day creative and social content in step with live results, on deadlines that left no room for a second pass.

  4. Pitch the partnership

    Designed sponsorship and sales decks that helped close brand partnerships for events around the world.

Speed or quality? The job was to refuse to pick one.
